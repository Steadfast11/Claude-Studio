# Phase 2 report — director fix + 62 characters + 65 actions + gallery

**Director (step 0).** `data/director/presets.json`: 13 beat types × 2–4 presets measured from ANOR's shots (lens, yaw, dist/height per 10 cm subject, lookY, move, focus pull, hold). `engine/director.js` places the camera in the subject's front half-space (≤ 70°) and the set's front arc, then checks start/mid/end: in frame, main subject ≥ 12 % of frame height, not cut, face visible; fail → next preset → pull back. Vertical/square frames come closer (same subject size); pairs are measured laterally so depth-staged two-shots stay tight. `compile.js` now tracks positions/headings over time (`posAt`, `yawAt`), beats call `c.cam(...)`; still characters turn to a 3/4 heading just before the cut. Reaction beats (discover, surprise, sad, talk) frame who + target; a prop alone is an insert ≤ 0.8 s; far targets → who walks closer first (followed by a tracking shot). chase/race/catch without a movable target → autofix swaps to `approach`. New beats: surprise, race, approach, perform, talk (13 total); any beat may carry `action`.
